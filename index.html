there is a coral network beneath Labuan Bajo, for whom the band of fishers check the voltage on the Biorock frames each morning
steel conducts current; seawater gives up its minerals; limestone accretes like accelerated time
the coral grows faster, but the reef also learned to store information and encode tide patterns and fish migrations in the growing crystalline structures, now legible as a living database
they speak the language of jiwa laut, the ocean's spirit, and they are its priests and students both
...
teenage girls are running around, laughter echoing through the humid evening air,
thumb drives around their necks in sacred pendants,
exchanging gifts and secrets to be opened in private, items existing nowhere else but within these pendants
under her mosquito net, a girl plugs her drive in, anticipating a secret scheme, or perhaps a curation of song and sound, or even a confession, the file encrypted with the scent of ylang-ylang that clings to her daster, untraceable; for her, only for her
...
an auntie is walking home at dusk, her sarong flickering in a humid breeze, electricity running down tedious lines, the embroidered batik patterns housing fiber-optic filaments that speak for mental messages
the light patterns are a silent broadcast between the women of the neighbourhood readying the town for a feast a story tomorrow
the children giggle because they do not understand yet
one day they will turn into aunties and share secrets with sarongs, and the children will giggle, because the children will not understand yet ...

there is a market stall with no price list, there is only the quiet appraisal of the bracelet on your wrist,
each strand a favour,
a thank you from yesterday, each strand a favour, a thank you for a time not yet met
“i would love four of these today. i’ll conjure up a big soup for you that’ll give you a week’s worth of meals and naps”
“soup! oh i love soup! i shall bring my lacquerware bowls to your home when you have me!”
we live in abundance!
we give in abundance!
community is currency because there is so much to spare
what you gift me today,i hope to double for my neighbour tomorrow,who triples it with immediate fervor, we live in abundance!

Ibu Lestari built and planted mangrove machine listeners along the coastline, their roots digging into the earth, breaching the ground, nutrients returned from a previous loan
when the low tide offers a mirror, the children gather around the lagoon to hear the Mangrove Song, as only the children are sung to, and we wait, for another season with and thanks to our Great Protectors
